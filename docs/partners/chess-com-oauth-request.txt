5. Briefly describe your application and why it requires Chess.com OAuth or Connected Board
   access. (required)

   Kingside is a native chess app for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple TV, built in Swift and SwiftUI.
   It has bots, pass-and-play, puzzles, and engine game review, and it works offline.

   Today Kingside uses only the Published-Data API. A player types their Chess.com username, and
   the app shows their public ratings and imports their recent games for review. It sends a
   User-Agent with contact details, makes requests one at a time, and caches responses.

   We are asking for OAuth so that players can sign in with their Chess.com account instead of
   typing a username. Signing in proves that the account is theirs, and it lets them see their own
   games and ratings in the app. If Chess.com offers it to partners, we would also like to let
   signed-in members play their Chess.com games from Kingside, under whatever rules and scopes you
   set. The app has a native board for touch, mouse, keyboard, and the Apple TV remote, which could
   help members who play on Apple TV or with accessibility needs.

   Kingside is not affiliated with Chess.com. It uses no Chess.com logos or artwork, and it gives
   no engine help during online games. It uses only the interfaces Chess.com provides.
