# CHESS Privacy Policy

CHESS is designed to keep local play local.

## Information stored on Apple TV

CHESS stores your saved and finished games, puzzle progress, and application preferences on the Apple TV so games can be resumed and reviewed. This information is not uploaded to the developer and can be removed by deleting the application.

## Network access

CHESS makes no network requests and collects no data. Games, bots, puzzles, and Game Review all run on the Apple TV without an internet connection.

## Tracking and advertising

CHESS does not include advertising, behavioral tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. It does not sell personal information.
