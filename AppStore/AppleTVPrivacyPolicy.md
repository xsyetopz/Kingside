# OpenChess Privacy Policy

OpenChess is designed to keep local play local.

## Information stored on your device

OpenChess stores your saved and finished games, puzzle progress, and application preferences on the device (Apple TV, iPhone, iPad, or Mac) so games can be resumed and reviewed. This information is not uploaded to the developer and can be removed by deleting the application.

## Game Center

You can sign in to Game Center from the Account screen; the app works without it. Game Center is an Apple service, and Apple handles your Game Center account under Apple's privacy policy. OpenChess uses GameKit to ask Apple whether you are signed in and to read your Game Center display name, which it shows on the Account screen. OpenChess does not send this information to the developer.

## Chess.com link (optional)

You can link a Chess.com account from the Account screen. OpenChess stores only the public Chess.com username that you type, on your device. It does not ask for a Chess.com password and does not use Chess.com sign-in.

When a Chess.com account is linked, OpenChess sends requests to Chess.com's public API (`https://api.chess.com/pub/`) to read that player's public profile, ratings, and recent games. You can import those games into the archive on your device. Chess.com receives the username and your device's IP address with each request and handles them under Chess.com's privacy policy. Unlinking removes the stored username.

## Network access

Games, bots, puzzles, and Game Review run on the device without an internet connection. OpenChess makes network requests only for Game Center sign-in (through Apple) and, if you link a Chess.com account, to the Chess.com public API. The developer runs no server and collects no data.

## Tracking and advertising

OpenChess does not include advertising, behavioral tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. It does not sell personal information.
